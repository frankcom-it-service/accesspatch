Controlled findings

CONTROLLED_BARRIER_EMAIL_NAME

Selector: #email

Before: The input has zero associated labels and no aria-label or aria-labelledby value; axe reports the label rule for #email.

Verified repair: The visible Email address text is programmatically associated with the email input and supplies its accessible name.

Strategy: associate_explicit_label

CONTROLLED_BARRIER_FOCUS_VISIBLE

Selector: .controlled-focus-defect

Before: The focused control computed outlineStyle=none, outlineWidth=3px, and boxShadow=none; no visible outline or box-shadow indicator was detected.

Verified repair: The keyboard-focused primary action has a clearly visible focus indicator without changing unrelated controls.

Strategy: restore_focus_visible

GPT-5.6 repair-plan summary

The supplied evidence identifies two controlled barriers in the completed checkout keyboard journey: the required email field lacks a programmatic association with its visible label, and the keyboard-focused primary action has no detected visible focus indicator. The plan limits each repair to its allowlisted file and affected area for a later controlled patch step.

The plan selected associate_explicit_label and restore_focus_visible. Deterministic templates—not model-generated code—implemented the isolated patch.
